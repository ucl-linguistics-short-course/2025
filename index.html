About this Programme

A short training programme organised by UCL Linguistics PhD students, introducing students of all levels to a broad range of linguistics topics.

This year we covered a wider range of topics than ever before — Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics, Morphology, and Psycholinguistics across three terms. These 5-session courses were free to attend and open to everyone. Whether you came to accompany your studies or out of curiosity, we hope you found something fascinating.

What Made This Programme Special

Free & Accessible

All courses were completely free to attend and open to everyone—from complete beginners to those already familiar with linguistics. No prerequisites, no barriers, just learning.

Expert-Led Sessions

Taught by UCL Linguistics PhD students and postdoctoral researchers who are passionate about sharing their expertise. Each 5-session course dived deep into specialised topics.

Certificates of Participation

Upon completion of each course, participants received a certificate of participation sent directly to their email—a great addition to any CV or academic portfolio.
